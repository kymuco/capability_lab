# Capability Lab Commercial Licensing

Capability Lab is made available publicly under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**.

The public license is intentionally a noncommercial grant. If a commercial use requires copyright or patent rights that are not granted by the public license, obtain a **separate written commercial license** from the Capability Lab licensor before exercising those rights.

## Commercial licensing boundary

```text
PUBLIC SOURCE ACCESS
!= COMMERCIAL USE PERMISSION

POLYFORM NONCOMMERCIAL GRANT
!= COMMERCIAL LICENSE
```

There is no automatic commercial-license exception based on:

- company revenue;
- employee or contractor count;
- startup stage;
- funding status;
- whether Capability Lab is customer-facing or used internally;
- whether the software is modified or used unchanged.

Do not assume that being a small company or early-stage project creates commercial license rights that are not present in the public license.

## Case-by-case licenses

The licensor may choose to offer a separate commercial license on terms appropriate to a specific use. That may include free or reduced-cost terms for selected independent developers, early-stage projects, research-aligned products, or other cases at the licensor's discretion.

Those possibilities are **not automatic public grants**. Commercial license rights from the Capability Lab licensor exist only when they are granted in a separate written agreement or other explicit written permission from the licensor.

## Rights provided by law

The PolyForm Noncommercial license expressly preserves fair-use rights that may exist under applicable law. This commercial-licensing overview does not narrow fair use or other rights that applicable law provides without permission from the licensor.

```text
SEPARATE COMMERCIAL LICENSE
!= WAIVER OF FAIR USE OR OTHER STATUTORY RIGHTS
```

## Requesting a commercial license

Contact the repository owner, [`@kymuco`](https://github.com/kymuco), through GitHub to request a commercial licensing discussion.

If using a public GitHub issue to make initial contact, do not include confidential business, security, personal, pricing, or contract information. Ask for a private channel first.

A useful initial request can identify:

- the organization or project;
- the intended use of Capability Lab;
- whether the use is internal, embedded in a product, offered as a service, or redistributed;
- the expected deployment scope;
- any timeline that matters.

## What this page is not

This page is an overview of the project's licensing model. It does not replace the public `LICENSE`, does not itself grant commercial rights, and is not a commercial license agreement.

If a separate commercial agreement is signed, that agreement controls the commercial rights it grants.

For the transition from the historical Apache-2.0 public checkpoint, see the canonical `LICENSE-HISTORY.md` record.
